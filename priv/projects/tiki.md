Tiki is an event management platform developed for the Computer Science Chapter
at KTH. It is used for both internal and public events. Tiki is custom built to
suit the needs of the organization, and includes functionality for payments,
tickets releases, and event management. Tiki was launched in 2025, and was used
to sell over 4000 tickets with a turnover over 1MSEK in its first year of use.

The system is [source-available](https://github.com/datasektionen/tiki) and
written in Elixir by me. For my work with Tiki, I was awarded the award _The
Great Delta_ by the chapter
([motivation](https://datasektionen.se/sektionen/storadeltat?lang=en#adrian-salamon-a-name-asalamon-a)).
To read more about Tiki, see its [about
page](https://tiki.datasektionen.se/about).
